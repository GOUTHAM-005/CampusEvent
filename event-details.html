About the Event

Tech Fest 2026 brings together students, innovators, and technology enthusiasts for a day of exploration and learning. Discover new ideas, showcase your skills, and connect with fellow students who share your passion for technology.

Participate in coding competitions, explore student project exhibitions, test your knowledge in technical quizzes, and attend inspiring talks and demonstrations.

Event Highlights

  • Coding competitions and problem-solving challenges
  • Student project exhibitions
  • Technical quizzes
  • Technology demonstrations
  • Guest talks and interactive sessions

Who Can Participate?

Students from all departments who are interested in technology, innovation, and creative problem-solving are welcome to participate.
